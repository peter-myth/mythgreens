About MYTH Greens Co. Ltd.

Bridging Farms & International Markets.

Strategically headquartered at Sri Muang Market in Ratchaburi, Thailand, we specialize in sourcing, grading, importing, and exporting premium fresh vegetables.

Who We Are

MYTH Greens Co. Ltd. is a dedicated fresh vegetable import and export company built on quality consistency, transparent trade relationships, and fast logistics. Situated right at the heart of Thailand’s central agricultural wholesale market (Lot 19-23, Sri Muang Market), we inspect and consolidate produce hours after harvest.

Whether supplying supermarkets, wholesale distributors, food processors, or HORECA buyers across Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and beyond, every shipment meets strict freshness and grading standards.

Why Partners Work With Us

  • ✅ Direct Hub Access: Located inside Sri Muang Market, Ratchaburi for peak freshness and competitive volume pricing.
  • ✅ Strict Quality Grading: Hand-sorted, cleaned, and packed to match destination market specifications.
  • ✅ End-to-End Cold Chain: Pre-cooling and refrigerated cross-border transport preserve shelf life.
  • ✅ Seamless Documentation: Full phytosanitary certificates, customs clearance, and import/export compliance.
